# Yatra-One
Yatra One

Yatra One is a smart tourism platform designed to enhance tourism experiences by promoting eco-tourism, cultural tourism, and digital accessibility for travelers.

PROJECT OVERVIEW:

The project was developed as part of Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2025 to address challenges in tourism discovery, local engagement, and cultural awareness.

FEATURES:
• Destination discovery and recommendations
• Eco-tourism and cultural tourism promotion
• Local guide and community integration
• Digital travel information
• Virtual tourism experiences
• User-friendly travel planning

TECHNOLOGIES/TOOLS USED:
• Research & Analysis
• Product Planning
• UI/UX Design
• Solution Architecture
• Documentation & Presentation

OBJECTIVES:
• Promote sustainable tourism
• Improve tourist accessibility to local information
• Support local communities and guides
• Enhance digital tourism experiences

TEAM PROJECT:
This Project was developed collaboratively as part of Smart India Hackathon 2025.
